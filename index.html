work

AI Scientist · Croptimistic Technology

Apr 2024 – present

Edmonton, Canada. Lead ML for SWAT CAM, a sprayer-mounted camera system for in-season crop analytics, covered by a 2026 U.S. patent.

  • Own the ML roadmap for a system deployed on 1.5M+ acres at 50M+ inferences/month, from research through gated release.
  • Moved the vision stack from hand-crafted features to transformer and DINOv3 backbones: segmentation, plant and dense counting, detection and row detection (96% IoU) for 9 crops plus weed detection.
  • Ran a 14-architecture segmentation benchmark that lifted boundary F1 0.87 → 0.94 and halved weed confusion; ablations showed the training recipe mattered more than architecture, so production got a cheaper model at equal accuracy.
  • Distilled a 343M-parameter DINOv3 teacher into a deployable student; used SAM3 as a labelling teacher; 1.28× inference speedup with torch.compile.
  • Rebuilt evaluation on leakage-free splits (ORB + RANSAC grouping of same-ground frames), exposing a 0.967 Dice inflated by near-duplicates; added weed-aware metrics, bootstrap CIs, paired tests and frozen hashed test sets.
  • Built the ML platform (FastAPI, React, SQLAlchemy) for annotation, training, evaluation and model promotion, with content-hashed data and models on S3, MLflow/DVC tracking and Docker/SQS inference workers.
  • Built a learning-to-rank recommender for management-zone maps from Sentinel-2 and topography (top pick matches experts 76% of the time) and a multimodal RAG over model outputs and millions of field images.
